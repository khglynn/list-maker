# Kevin's Podcast Library — Taste Signal

Transcribed from 5 library screenshots (Pocket Casts, captured 2026-05-31). These are shows Kevin *already subscribes to* — the single best signal of taste for making recommendations. This is NOT a list of mental-health shows; it's the baseline of "what good looks like" to Kevin across genres.

> Note: the source screenshots could not be saved as image binaries in this session. Drop the raw PNGs into `screenshots/` if you want them version-controlled.

## What this library reveals (pattern summary)

- **Heavy bias toward tightly-produced narrative journalism & reported audio.** In The Dark, Serial, 99% Invisible, Heavyweight, Search Engine, Post Reports, The Improvement Association, Aftermath (Anthrax). These are fact-checked, sourced, editorially-backed shows — exactly the "reporting backbone" Kevin says he requires.
- **Smart explainer / question-answering done rigorously.** Search Engine (PJ Vogt), Science Vs (literally a fact-checking show), 99% Invisible, The Allusionist, Immaterial. Note the contrast Kevin draws with "No Such Thing As A Fish" — he likes the *format* but rejects un-sourced, credulous versions of it.
- **Tech & AI, analyst-grade.** Hard Fork, Sharp Tech (Ben Thompson), The a16z Show, Practical AI, The AI Daily Brief, Imagining Arc (Browser Company).
- **News & politics, institutional.** Pod Save America/World, Political Gabfest, The Political Scene, Post Reports, FiveThirtyEight (PODCAST-19), The Headlines, Amicus, America Actually (Astead Herndon), The Gray Area.
- **Vox / NYT / NPR / Pushkin / Gimlet production houses recur** — a preference for outlets with editorial standards over independent shoot-from-the-hip shows.
